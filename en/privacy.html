Privacy Policy

Effective 18 Sep 2026 · Last updated 18 Sep 2026

Parked Location (주차 위치) processes personal information as described below to protect users in accordance with applicable law.

1. Information we collect

  • Location — Used to show a parking pin on the map and to save the spot when the car’s Bluetooth disconnects. Floor and zone are not inferred from location. We do not store location on a server long-term.
  • Camera & photos — Used only with permission to photograph parking pillar signs so floor and zone can be recorded. Photos stay on this device.
  • Microphone & speech recognition — Used only with permission to record floor and zone by voice.
  • Bluetooth connection state — Used to detect when the car disconnects so the parking location can be saved.
  • Parking records — Coordinates, floor/zone, and timestamps are stored only on this device (app group).
  • Device & diagnostics — Firebase Crashlytics / Google Analytics may collect crash and usage stats (anonymous, aggregated). (Release / TestFlight)
  • Advertising ID — May be used to serve Google AdMob ads.

We do not collect account information such as name, phone number, or email.

2. How we use information

  • Save parking location, show it on the map, and help you return to the car
  • Record floor and zone (photo, voice, or manual entry)
  • Show parking info in widgets
  • Analyze errors and improve quality
  • Show in-app ads

3. Third parties / processors

The following may process information to provide the service.

  • Apple (device features such as maps, location, and speech recognition)
  • Google (Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics, AdMob)

Each provider processes data under its own policies.

4. Retention

  • Location and parking records: Kept on the device and removed when you delete the record or the app. Records may reset if you move far from the saved location.
  • Pillar photos: Removed when you delete them in the app or delete the app.
  • Crashlytics / Analytics / ads: Follow each provider’s policy.

5. Your choices

  • Location, camera, and microphone: You can revoke access anytime in iPhone Settings.
  • Parking records and photos: Delete them in the app, or delete the app.
  • Ad tracking: Limit it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: kjpgy@naver.com

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective and last-modified dates on this page. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
