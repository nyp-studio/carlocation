FAQ

Parking location isn’t saved automatically

On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Parked Location (주차 위치) and allow Always. Even if the app is closed, it can save the spot when the car’s Bluetooth disconnects.

Floor and zone don’t show on the map

Floor and zone are not estimated from GPS. Photograph the parking pillar sign, say something like “B1 A4”, or enter them in the app.

My parking record disappeared

If you move far from the saved spot, the record may reset. If the app asks whether to restore the previous location, you can restore it.

I see ads

Some screens may show a Google AdMob banner to buy the developer a coffee.
